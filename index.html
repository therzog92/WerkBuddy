WERKBUDDY

Flash your desk from the browser — no installs, no code.

  • Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  • Click Connect & Flash.
  • Pick the USB-Serial device in the popup.
  • Wait for the progress bar to fill — the desk reboots on its own.

This flashes the factory image, which may not be the very latest release. After setup, check Settings → Network → Updates on the desk.
