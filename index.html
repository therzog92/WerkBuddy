WERKBUDDY

Flash your WerkBuddy from the browser — no installs, no code.

  • Plug the board in with a USB-C data cable.
  • Click Connect & Flash.
  • Pick the USB-SERIAL CH340 device in the popup.
  • Wait for the progress bar to fill — the WerkBuddy reboots on its own.

We only show the WerkBuddy's CH340 chip — Bluetooth and other entries are hidden.

This flashes the factory image, which may not be the very latest release. After setup, check Settings → Network → Updates on the WerkBuddy.
